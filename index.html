Welcome to Queen Sheeks

Rooted in Jamaica. Made with Love. Created to Bring People Together.

Queen Sheeks is more than a food experience. It’s a celebration of Jamaican heritage, bold Caribbean flavours, heartfelt hospitality and the joy of sharing unforgettable moments around the table.

Today, that vision is coming to life through our restaurant pop-ups, thoughtfully crafted dining experiences and authentic Jamaican cooking, as we work towards opening our permanent home.

Every dish carries a little piece of Jamaica, prepared with passion, pride and a whole lot of love.

Our Story

Rooted in Jamaica. Inspired by Family. Made with Love.

Every great dream has a beginning. Mine began long before the first Queen Sheeks restaurant pop-up.

Born in Kingston, Jamaica, my love for food, family and the traditions that bring people together began at an early age.

Even as a child, I dreamed of opening my own restaurant — somewhere I could celebrate Jamaican cuisine, share the flavours of my heritage and make people feel welcome through food.

When I moved to England at just seven years old, that dream travelled with me. Although my surroundings changed, my Jamaican roots remained a part of who I was and the future I wanted to create.

More Than a Dream

A Family Legacy

Three generations. One family. A dream built with love.

Family has always been at the heart of Queen Sheeks.

My father and grandfather are an important part of the inspiration behind my journey. Their love, influence and the values they instilled in me have helped shape the woman and chef I am today.

Queen Sheeks represents more than my ambition to open a restaurant. It represents where I come from, the people who have inspired me and the legacy I hope to build through food, culture and hospitality.

I want to create a place where people feel welcomed, where memories are made around the table and where every dish carries the warmth and love that inspired this dream.

The Journey So Far

From a Childhood Dream to an Award-Winning Journey

In January 2026, Queen Sheeks began bringing my restaurant vision to life through pop-up dining experiences, allowing guests to enjoy the food and hospitality I hope to offer one day in a permanent restaurant.

Along the way, Queen Sheeks has also brought Jamaican and Caribbean-inspired flavours to corporate workplaces, private events and cultural celebrations.

In September 2026, I proudly reached the final of the StrEATfood Awards and won 3rd Place, Dish of the Year — a milestone that reminded me just how far this childhood dream has come.

Every experience, every customer and every opportunity continues to shape the journey.

Our First Experience

Caribbean Seasoning Masterclass

Discover the foundations of Caribbean flavour.

Join award-winning Chef Roshika Forrest for a hands-on masterclass exploring two essential elements of Caribbean cooking: Caribbean green seasoning and authentic Jamaican jerk paste.

Learn how fresh herbs, spices and aromatics come together to create the bold, balanced flavours at the heart of Caribbean cuisine. During this interactive session, you’ll create your own seasonings, discover how to use them in everyday cooking and take home the jars you’ve prepared.

What You’ll Learn
  • How to make Caribbean green seasoning from scratch
  • How to prepare authentic Jamaican jerk paste
  • How to select and combine key herbs and spices
  • How to balance heat and flavour
  • Practical ways to use your homemade seasonings
